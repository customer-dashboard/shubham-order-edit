# Order Edit Pro - Merchant Guide

Empower your customers to fix order mistakes directly from the Order Status page. Reduce support tickets, prevent unnecessary cancellations, and improve customer satisfaction by providing a seamless self-service order editing experience.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Self-Service Order Editing
Give your customers the power to update their orders without waiting for a support agent.
*   **Shipping Address Updates:** Customers can fix typos or change their delivery address after checkout.
*   **Phone Number Editing:** Ensure delivery drivers can always reach the customer by allowing phone number corrections.
*   **Order Line Items:** Customers can change quantities or remove items from their order if they change their mind.
*   **Add Products:** Allow customers to add more items to their existing order, increasing your Average Order Value (AOV).

### 2. Post-Purchase Engagement
*   **Apply Discount Codes:** Did a customer forget to use a coupon? They can apply it after the order is placed.
*   **Invoice Download:** Customers can download their professional PDF invoices directly from the order status page.
*   **Delivery Instructions:** Provide a field for customers to leave specific notes for the delivery driver (e.g., "Leave at the back gate").

---

## 🛡️ Advanced Restrictions & Controls

Take full control over when and who can edit orders with our powerful restriction engine.

### ⏳ Time Limit Restriction
Prevent edits after an order has likely been processed or shipped.
*   Set a window (e.g., 30 minutes, 2 hours, or 1 day) during which editing is allowed.
*   Once the time limit expires, the editing options automatically disappear for that order.

### 🏷️ Order Tag Restriction
Only allow editing for orders that meet specific criteria.
*   **Use Case:** Only allow edits for orders tagged with "Pending Review" or "Pre-order".
*   **Matching Logic:** Choose between "Match Any Tag" (Allow if at least one tag matches) or "Match All Tags" (Allow only if all specified tags are present).

### 👥 Customer Tag Restriction
Reward your best customers with exclusive editing privileges.
*   **Use Case:** Only allow "VIP" or "Wholesale" members to modify their orders.
*   **Matching Logic:** Flexible "Any" or "All" tag matching for customer profiles.

## 📊 Analytics & Insights

Stay informed about how your customers are interacting with their orders.
*   **Overview Dashboard:** See total edited orders and active features at a glance.
*   **Trend Charts:** Visualize order editing activity over the last 30 days.
*   **Recent Activity Log:** View a real-time list of which orders were edited and what changes were made.

---

## 🛠️ How to Setup

1.  **Enable the App:** Go to the **Settings** tab and toggle the main "Enable" switch to ON.
2.  **Configure Features:** Enable the specific editing features you want to offer (e.g., Address Editing, Adding Products).
3.  **Set Restrictions:** Define your Time Limits or Tag-based restrictions to protect your fulfillment process.
4.  **Activate Extension:** Click the "Open checkout settings" button on the Dashboard to add the **Order Edit Block** to your Shopify Order Status page using the Shopify web editor.

## 💡 Benefits for Your Store
*   **Lower Support Costs:** Drastically reduce "Where is my order?" and "I put the wrong address" emails.
*   **Higher Customer Trust:** Customers feel more confident shopping when they know they can fix minor mistakes.
